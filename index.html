Meet the Safe Mushrooms

Chanterelle
Gailene

Golden treasure - easy to spot, hard to confuse.
Frilly edges, pleasant apricot smell.

Porcini
Baravika

THE KING
Rare and most prized! Brown cap, thick white stem, pores underneath.
The golden treasure of the forest!

Russula
Bērzlape

Common friend - red/brown cap, white gills.
Brittle, crumbly flesh. Safe and reliable!

Skirt on Stem

Look for a skirt or ring around the stem—could be poisonous!

White Spots

White spots appear - Death Cap signature!

Pure White Gills

If the gills are pure white, double-check before picking.

Bright Colors

Bright, unfamiliar, or very unusual colors = hands off!

Bulb at Base

Beware the bulb at the base, hidden under soil.
